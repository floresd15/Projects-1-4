Establishing my Availability and Booking Clients

Deciding Availability

  1. School Schedule

    I block out slots meant for classes and extra curriculars. I also block extra time during exam season to study.

  2. Days off

    Making sure I give myself time to rest.

  3. Best for clients

    Picking times where I am most likely to get booked

Posting Schedule

I post my availability every 2 weeks on Sundays On Instagram. 24 hours before my schedule goes live, I send out a countdown so clients are prepared to book.

Answering Messages

I answer messages first come first style. I make sure that the date they want is available, then I give them a set price and request a deposit fee to secure their spot. Once that is sent I give them my address. A day before the appointment I send out a message confirming their presence.
