Answering Messages

I answer messages first come first served. I confirm the date, send a price, and request a deposit to secure the spot.

I answer messages first come first style. I make sure that the date they want is available, then I give them a set price and request a deposit fee to secure their spot. Once that is sent I give them my address. A day before the appointment I send out a message confirming their presence.
